# Using the help

`F1` opens the help page of what you are looking at: the dialog on top, the
mode of the active panel, or the folder the panel lists. With the cursor on
a row such as `/diagnosis`, `/timeline` or `logs`, it opens the page about
that row. Where no page fits, it opens the [index](index.md).

The help window sits over the screen. Closing it returns to the dialog or
view you came from, as you left it: a dialog keeps the text you typed.

## Links

Words in a cyan box are links to other pages. The selected link is white
on blue. `Enter` follows it, and `F3` comes back.

## Keys

- `Up`, `Down` scroll by one line; `PgUp`, `PgDn` and `Space` by a page.
- `Home`, `End` go to the top and the bottom of the page.
- `Tab` and `Shift-Tab` select the next and the previous link.
- `Enter` or `Right` follows the selected link. A click on a link follows it too.
- `F3`, `Left` or `Backspace` go back to the previous page.
- `F2` opens the index of all pages.
- `F1` opens this page.
- `F10`, `Esc` or `q` close the help.

Function keys also work as `Esc` and a digit: `Esc 1` is `F1`, `Esc 0` is
`F10`.

## Limits

- The text wraps at 76 columns, however wide the terminal is.
- The pages describe this build of kc; they ship inside the binary.
